# Company marks

- Microsoft: four-square symbol in its four brand colors.
- IBM: horizontal Eye–Bee–M rebus from https://www.ibm.com/design/language/1ee5d3842f1ef9a4026cbb5f6f41ba25/rebus-light.svg (https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ibm-logos/rebus/). Background and outer whitespace removed; original symbol proportions and internal spacing preserved.
- Lockheed Martin: star group from https://www.lockheedmartin.com/content/dam/lockheed-martin/general/lm-logo.svg ; wordmark omitted for the small mark beside the company name.

Marks are decorative beside visible company names and identify employment history. Microsoft stays full color; Lockheed Martin uses blue in light mode and white in dark mode.

The light-theme IBM rebus uses the original single-color vector in IBM Blue 70 (`#0043ce`), rather than recoloring the classic multicolor artwork. The classic multicolor variant remains in dark mode. Both variants share the same viewBox and dimensions.
