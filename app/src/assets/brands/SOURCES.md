# Company marks

- Microsoft: four-square symbol, rendered as a monochrome vector.
- IBM: 8-bar paths from https://github.com/IBM/bamoe-docs/blob/main/images/ibm-8-bar-logo.svg ; background and surrounding whitespace removed.
- Lockheed Martin: star group from https://www.lockheedmartin.com/content/dam/lockheed-martin/general/lm-logo.svg ; wordmark omitted for the small mark beside the company name.

Marks are decorative beside visible company names and use the text color for light and dark themes. They identify employment history.
