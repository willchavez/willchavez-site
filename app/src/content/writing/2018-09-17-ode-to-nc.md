I’m writing this from the Raleigh-Durham airport, omw to start a new life in San Francisco. My cat Wormy is on my lap in this little mesh carrier. All my memories of Raleigh and of Durham are hitting me rn. The first day I got there my best friend Danny got all his friends to go to this spot called Buku. We hit up a little tequila bar before, and then the nearby beer garden, and I met with Mari, Ellie, Taha, Maya, and some other fam. I had no idea that these people would be some of the closest people in my life.

One time Danny threw a little dinner at his place, and we ate some ceviche and some of his lemony-ass guac (which is bomb). I remember there being a distinct moment when we were all sitting around the couch, some of the friends were on sitting on the floor, and we were probably arguing about something political or some dumb shit Danny might have said. But in that moment I felt something that I hadn’t felt in a long time, I felt that I had a small family. Fuck, I’m tearing up right now. That was early 2017.

During that time, I went through a little break-up with a friend, and I was constantly bugging my friends with existential/romantic questions about life and love. Not too many were there when I was dealing with my issues, but they were always there for me, to listen to me, to let me know when I was stuck in a loop, to give me a reality check when I needed it. For the Fourth of July, we lit some shitty fireworks and had a little get together at Mar’s place.

When Danny left to NY, we got shit-faced and Mari, and Taha rescued me from being stoned outside. At the same time I met Chelsea and Chris, two really down-to-earth homies. For my bday in 2017, we went out to Motorco and savagely ate cake with forks only, no plates. For Easter of this year, we went to church, had a little Easter egg hunt outside Mar’s house, got drunk off mimosas, and ended up eating a half-eaten cake from a Greek restaurant. Exploring Durham during Moogfest.

Meeting random people at concerts that also work at IBM. Going to Cat’s Cradle for literally every concert. Also S/O to my friends at work, Cheyenne and Tony. Feeling very thankful for **every single one of those moments**. My friends helped me learn to be myself around people. I owe them so much.

> Without these friendships — life, what cauchemar!” — T.S. Eliot
