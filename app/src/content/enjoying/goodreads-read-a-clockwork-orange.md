Finally got around to reading this. It took a minute to get used to the language, and Alex is a controversial antagonist and anti-hero. The rhythm and onomatopoeia of the language helps with the world-building. Overall, I loved that this book made me think about topics like free will and ideology. I understand and respect the hype.
