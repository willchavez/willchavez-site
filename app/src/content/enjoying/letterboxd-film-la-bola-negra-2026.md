<blockquote>“This country has too many love stories buried in the fields.”</blockquote>

Enjoyed this one a lot! It can be a bit hard to follow at times, but the plot resolves beautifully. Part of me kept thinking of last year’s [The Secret Agent](/enjoying/letterboxd-film-the-secret-agent-2025); there are some real parallels in the storytelling. Theatrical, poetic, and worth sticking with. It stayed with me well after the credits.
