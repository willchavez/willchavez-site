Longest episode of couples therapy.

Loved this documentary. It reminded me of a line from Charles Mingus: “Touch my beloved’s thought while her world’s affluence crumbles at my feet.”

Something tender and unsettling about watching two people cling to each other as their world falls apart. At times, it’s difficult to distinguish love from denial, devotion from delusion.
