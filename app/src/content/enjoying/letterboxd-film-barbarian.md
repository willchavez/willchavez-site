This review may contain spoilers.

The way this story is told is so fascinating! I saw Weapons before seeing this and I love the way the director splits up both films. In Barbarian, and in Weapons, the different perspectives helped me understand it's deeper themes, which can be hard for me to do with horror because I often get lost in the fear of the visual and auditory elements. Honestly, such a great film.
