Adored reading this book. Sometimes some of the references were a little lost on me, but I loved the passion and gratitude felt by Hanif. Hanif blended his personal life stories in with the ATCQ history, and black history, and let us step into his perspective on this beloved rap group. Some of the letters to each of the members had me on verge of tears. Ultimately, I left this book feeling incredibly thankful for all the artists in my life.
