I enjoyed this film! I think it starts off a little shaky, but it sticks the landing. At first you’re introduced to Hart and you’re not sure why the dialogue moves so fast, but as the film goes on you appreciate it more and more, and you start to see how deeply sensitive and romantic he is, despite the pain he’s carrying.
